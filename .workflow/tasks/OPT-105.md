# OPT-105 Pricing library: BSM price, greeks, implied vol, structure payoff and scenario grid, receipts
Objective: Pure functions, no I/O, no model. bsm_price, bsm_greeks, implied_vol (bracketing solver with explicit failure), `Structure` limited to the approved set in OPTIONS_M2_PLAN (validator rejects anything else and any unlimited-risk combination), payoff_at_expiry, contractual_bounds (max loss, max gain, breakevens exact from legs), scenario_grid(structure, quotes, as_of, horizon, spot/iv/time steps, r, fees) with P&L per one contract-set per cell, mid for valuation, ask to buy and bid to sell at entry and the reverse at exit, assumptions listed. All outputs are CalculationReceipts.
Current behaviour: none.
Allowed files: options-research/src/options_research/options/{pricing,structures,scenarios}.py, options-research/tests/test_pricing.py, options-research/config/default.toml ([pricing] risk_free_rate placeholder, fees_per_contract labelled FIXTURE, scenario steps), .workflow/handoffs/OPT-105.md
Forbidden: numpy/scipy (stdlib math only); probabilities or expected value; vendor greeks inside our pricing.
Acceptance checks:
- Put-call parity to 1e-8; implied_vol(bsm_price(sigma)) recovers sigma to 1e-6; price below intrinsic -> ImpliedVolUnavailable.
- Bull call spread: max loss = net debit + fees, max gain = width - net debit - fees, exact breakeven; credit spread symmetric; long call max loss = premium + fees.
- Validator rejects a naked short call and a ratio spread.
- Grid deterministic (same receipt ids); expiry cells equal payoff minus entry cost; a cell with an unusable leg quote is None.
- Zero vol and T = 0 return intrinsic without exceptions.
Risk: L3 (numbers)
Stop conditions: pricing American options as European without the caveat in receipt assumptions.
Context: docs/CLAUDE_SETUP.md "Calculation tools", "Pricing specialist".
